How CitySense works

From a report
to a response.

CitySense brings related reports together so teams can understand a problem, coordinate work, and share progress.

  1. 01

    Tell us what happened

    Describe the problem and optionally add a photo or location.

  2. 02

    Smart assessment

    CitySense analyzes the report and recommends a category, priority, and department.

  3. 03

    Bring related reports together

    Related reports can contribute to the same underlying incident.

  4. 04

    Follow progress

    Response teams share updates until the problem is resolved.
