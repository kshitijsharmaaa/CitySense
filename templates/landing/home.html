From a report to a response

CitySense brings related reports together so teams can understand a problem, coordinate work, and share progress.

1. Tell us what happened

Describe the problem and add a photo or map location if it helps.

2. Get a smart assessment

CitySense suggests the type of problem, its priority, and the department that may be able to help.

3. Bring related reports together

Reports about the same local problem can be grouped so response teams can see the full picture.

4. Follow progress

Response teams share updates until the problem is resolved.
