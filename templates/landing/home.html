Beyond Generic CRUD Complaints

Traditional portals store disconnected complaints. CitySense creates continuous civic intelligence by clustering reports into unified civic incidents.

1. Unstructured Report

Citizens report issues using plain text, optional photos, and precise GPS location markers.

2. AI Triage

AI suggests problem categories, severity priority, department routing, and concise executive summaries.

3. Incident Aggregation

Spatial and semantic analysis aggregates redundant duplicate reports into a single actionable civic incident.

4. Resolution & Tracking

Administrators assign departments and execute resolution with transparent status updates for citizens.
